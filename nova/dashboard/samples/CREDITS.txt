NOVA voice samples

Rendered from Surge XT (https://surge-synthesizer.github.io/), the free and
open-source synthesizer (GPL-3.0), using patches from its factory and
contributed libraries:

  angel    "Breathy Ooh" by John Valentine
  ooh      "Ooh" (Surge XT factory)

One note every three semitones, mono, 24 kHz MP3. NOVA pitches each sample to
the nearest note it needs. Everything else in the soundscapes (bowls, handpan,
chimes, pad, bass) is synthesized live.
